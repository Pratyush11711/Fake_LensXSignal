How the dual pipeline reads multimodal evidence

Two disciplined, auditable detection pipelines operating in tandem — lexical statistical NLP and deep spatio-temporal computer vision.

Stream A

NLP Lexical Pipeline

01
Text Normalization

HTML/URL stripping, lowercase normalization, optional Reuters dateline isolation.

02
TF-IDF Vectorization

Unigrams + Bigrams, 5000 max features, sublinear term-frequency scaling.

03
Logistic Regression

Linear decision boundary with explicit coefficient explainability.

04
Calibrated Probability

Sigmoid activation mapping to binary REAL (0) vs FAKE (1).

Stream B

SIGNAL Deepfake Video Pipeline

01
YuNet Face Tracking

OpenCV ONNX facial detection bounding boxes & 5-point landmark extraction.

02
Affine Alignment & ROI

Facial rotation compensation and consecutive mouth region crops (mean 0.421, std 0.165).

03
Temporal Neural Inference

Pretrained Vision Transformer (ViT) or ResNet-18 + Multi-Scale Temporal CNN (MS-TCN).

04
3-Tier Forensic Policy

Calibrated boundaries: Likely Authentic (<0.40), Inconclusive (0.40–0.90), Manipulation (≥0.90).

exhibit 04 — academic rigor

Checking the result isn't a trick

REAL articles in ISOT carry Reuters datelines. We tested whether the model leans on that metadata instead of the story itself.

Configuration Accuracy Precision Recall F1
Original text (benchmark) 99.14% 99.35% 98.77% 99.06%
Reuters datelines stripped 98.33% 98.92% 97.40% 98.16%
Strict deduplication (0 title/prefix overlap) 98.90% 99.38% 98.19% 98.78%
Reading: After removing the major Reuters/dateline source artifact and testing stricter duplicate controls, performance remained high at 98.33–98.90%. This confirms the classifier learns authentic lexical patterns beyond simple publisher tags.

exhibit 07

Scientific limits & forensic boundaries

NLP Classification Scope

Dataset-based stylistic and lexical classification. It models resemblance to ISOT benchmark patterns — it does not query live fact-checking databases.

Deepfake Video Boundaries

High video compression, extreme low lighting, or rapid motion blur can elevate forensic anomaly scores. A high score flags anomalies for human review, not absolute proof.

Multimodal Synthesis

By cross-referencing article text and accompanying video evidence, investigators can detect coordinated multi-vector synthetic disinformation campaigns.
